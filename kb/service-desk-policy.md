# Service desk policy and SLAs

## Priorities and response targets
| Priority | Meaning | First response | Resolution target |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Business down or security incident (outage, compromised account, lost device) | 15 minutes | 4 hours |
| P2 | Team or service degraded (printer down for many, VPN failing for several users) | 1 hour | 1 business day |
| P3 | Single user issue with a workaround | 4 hours | 3 business days |
| P4 | Request or question (access, how-to, new software) | 1 business day | 5 business days |

## Automation and AI assistance
The AI copilot drafts answers from this knowledge base only. Replies are sent automatically only for low-risk P3/P4 questions where the copilot is confident and an independent check agrees the answer is fully supported by the knowledge base. Security incidents, access changes, P1/P2 issues, and any ticket containing personal or sensitive information always go to a human.
