# Office Wi-Fi and network

- Staff network: "Office-Staff" (WPA2-Enterprise, sign in with your work account).
- Guest network: "Office-Guest". Password is displayed at reception and changes monthly. Guests cannot reach file shares or printers.
- Cannot connect: forget the network and reconnect; check the date and time on your device; restart Wi-Fi.
- Internet is slow for everyone: raise a P2 ticket. If it is only you, move closer to the access point and close large uploads or syncs.
- Never plug personal routers or switches into office wall ports.
