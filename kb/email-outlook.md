# Email and Outlook

- Outlook keeps asking for a password: sign out of Office apps, clear saved credentials in Windows Credential Manager, then sign in again (MFA prompt expected).
- Mailbox full: archive old mail with the Online Archive, empty Deleted Items, and remove large attachments.
- Shared mailbox missing: shared mailboxes appear automatically about an hour after access is granted. Restart Outlook.
- Calendar not syncing on phone: remove and re-add the account in the Outlook mobile app.
- Sending large files: share a link from the file share or OneDrive instead of attaching files larger than 20 MB.
