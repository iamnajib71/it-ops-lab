# Passwords and multi-factor authentication (MFA)

## Resetting a forgotten password
1. Go to the self-service reset portal from the sign-in page and click "Forgot password".
2. Verify with your registered authenticator app or mobile number.
3. Choose a passphrase of at least 14 characters. Do not reuse old passwords.
IT staff will never ask for your password by email, chat or phone.

## Account locked out
Accounts lock for 15 minutes after 5 failed attempts. Wait 15 minutes, then try again carefully. Phones with an old saved password often cause repeat lockouts; update the password in mail apps.

## New phone or lost authenticator
Raise an Access ticket. IT will verify your identity on a video call before resetting MFA. A lost phone is a security issue; mention it so IT can sign out other sessions.

## Suspicious email asking for your password
Do not click. Use "Report phishing" in Outlook or forward it to IT, then delete it. If you already entered your password, change it immediately and raise a P1 Security ticket.
